Eleventh Lesson

Nullifiers of Prayer

The nullifiers of prayer are eight:

1. Speaking intentionally while knowing and remembering
Speech that is not from the prayer. If one speaks out of forgetfulness or ignorance then the prayer is valid.

2. Laughing
Smiling does not invalidate the prayer.

3. Eating

4. Drinking

5. Uncovering the private parts
The 'Awrah (private parts that must be covered).

6. Turning away from the Qiblah excessively
Turning the chest away from the direction of the Qiblah.

7. Excessive continuous movement without need
Many movements done continuously and not separated. Three movements in a row is considered excessive.

8. Invalidation of Wudu
If one's Wudu is invalidated during the prayer.

The Ruling on Movement in Prayer

Obligatory Movement

Covering one's private parts if they become uncovered. Turning towards the Qiblah if one turns away from it.

Recommended Movement

Moving to fill a gap in the row. Moving to get closer to the Imam.

Permissible Movement

Light movement that does not break concentration. Scratching or adjusting clothes briefly.

Prohibited Movement

Excessive movement that takes away from the prayer. Continuous fidgeting without need.

Prostration of Forgetfulness

The prostration of forgetfulness is done in three cases:

1. Addition (Ziyaadah)
If one adds an extra pillar such as an extra Ruku' or Sujud, one should do the prostration of forgetfulness after the Tasleem.

2. Omission (Naqs)
If one omits an obligation such as the first Tashahhud, one should do the prostration of forgetfulness before the Tasleem.

3. Doubt (Shakk)
If one doubts whether they prayed three or four rak'ahs, they should build upon certainty (the lesser number) and do the prostration of forgetfulness before the Tasleem.

The prostration of forgetfulness consists of two prostrations like the prostrations of prayer with the Tasbeeh. It is followed by sitting and the Tasleem.
