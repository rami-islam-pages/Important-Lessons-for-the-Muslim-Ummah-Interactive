Explanation of Lesson Ten

Tenth Lesson

Recommended Acts of Prayer

What is other than the conditions, pillars, and obligations is recommended. The one who leaves out the recommended acts is not sinful nor does one have to do the prostration of forgetfulness. Some of the recommended acts are:

1. The Opening Supplication

سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَى جَدُّكَ، وَلَا إِلَهَ غَيْرُكَ

Transliteration: Subhaanak-Allaahumma wa bihamdik, wa tabaarakasmuk, wa ta'aalaa jadduk, wa laa ilaaha ghayruk.

Translation: Glory be to You, O Allah, and praise be to You. Blessed is Your Name. Exalted is Your Majesty. There is no deity worthy of worship besides You.

2. Seeking refuge before recitation
Saying: "A'oodhu billaahi minash-Shaytaanir-Rajeem" (I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan).

3. Saying Bismillah
Saying "Bismillaahir-Rahmaanir-Raheem" before Al-Fatiha.

4. Saying Ameen
After completing Al-Fatiha.

5. Reciting a Surah after Al-Fatiha
In the first two rak'ahs of the obligatory prayers and in all rak'ahs of the recommended prayers.

6. Reciting aloud
For the Imam in Fajr, Jumu'ah, the first two rak'ahs of Maghrib and Isha, Taraweeh, Witr, and Eid prayers.

7. Saying after "Rabbana wa lakal-Hamd":
"Mil'as-samaawaati wa mil'al-ard, wa mil'a maa shi'ta min shay'in ba'd" (Filling the heavens and filling the earth and filling whatever You wish after that).

8. More than one Tasbeeh in Ruku' and Sujud
The first is obligatory; additional ones are recommended.

9. More than once saying "Rabb Ighfir-lee"
Between the two prostrations.

10. Making the head level with the back in Ruku'

11. Keeping the arms away from the sides
The stomach away from the thighs in prostration and keeping the thighs away from the legs.

12. Raising the forearms off the ground in prostration

13. Sitting in Iftiraash between the two prostrations and in the first Tashahhud
And sitting in Tawarruk in the last Tashahhud in three and four rak'ah prayers.

14. Placing the hands on the thighs
With fingers spread between the two prostrations. In the Tashahhud, the right hand is in a fist with the index finger pointing and moving during supplication. The left hand is spread on the left thigh.

15. Looking at the place of prostration
Except during the Tashahhud when pointing with the index finger; one looks at it.

16. Turning to the right and left when making the Tasleem

Raising the Hands

The hands are raised in four places:

  1. With the opening Takbeer
  2. When going down for Ruku'
  3. When rising from Ruku'
  4. When standing after the first Tashahhud
