Fifth Lesson

Ihsaan

The pillar of Ihsaan: It is to worship Allah as if you see Him, even though we do not see Him in this life, He sees us.

Ihsaan - It is one pillar which is divided into two levels:

Worship of Mushaahadah

(As if one sees Him) out of love, desiring, and yearning for that which is with Allah while fearing Him at the same time. For example: the worship of the Prophets and Messengers - peace be upon them-. The Prophet said: "…Should I not be a thankful slave!" It is possible for other than them to reach this level as well.

Worship of Muraaqabah

(Of the one who knows that he is being observed). It is a worship out of fear and fleeing. A Muslim does not go outside of this circle.
