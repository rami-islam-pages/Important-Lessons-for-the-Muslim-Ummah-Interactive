Explanation of Lesson Seven

Seventh Lesson

Pillars of Prayer

The pillars are fourteen:

1. Standing if able to do so
If one is unable to stand then s/he would pray sitting down. If s/he is unable to sit then lying down on one's side facing the Qiblah and if unable then on the back with the legs facing the Qiblah. If one is unable to move the body then with the eyes. If one is unable to do that then with the heart. Standing is a pillar for the one who is able to stand. The two prostrations are a pillar, and every pillar should be fulfilled if one is able. The pillars are only excused for those who are unable to fulfill them.

2. Takbiratul-Ihraam
It is the Opening Takbeer.

3. Reading Al-Fatiha
Every rak'ah of obligatory and recommended prayers. There is no prayer without it.

4. Ruku'
Ruku' is bowing.

5. Rising from Ruku'

6. Standing straight after Ruku'
As long as needed; until one's bones settle.

7. Sujud (Prostration)
Bowing down on seven bones: The forehead which includes the nose, both hands, both knees, and the toes of both feet.

8. Rising from Sujud

9. Sitting between the two prostrations
How one sits is according to one's choice. However, Iftiraash is recommended as a first choice. Iftiraash is sitting on the left foot with the toes of the right foot facing the Qiblah.

10. Tranquility in all actions
Tranquility is a must in every pillar; standing, sitting, Ruku', rising from Ruku', prostration, sitting between the two prostrations, and sitting in the final Tashahhud. The Prophet ‫ﷺ‬ commanded the one who prayed incorrectly: "Then prostrate until you are at ease while prostrating."

11. The last Tashahhud

12. Sitting for the last Tashahhud
Tawarruk is recommended for those who pray three or four rak'ahs. It is obligatory for those who are led in prayer to sit when the Imam sits unless one is praying sitting down and wishes to stand for the Tashahhud then one should do so.

13. Sending Salawaat on the Prophet ‫ﷺ‬
For the last Tashahhud.

14. The two Tasleems
With two Tasleems; one to the right and one to the left. Saying "As-Salaamu Alaykum wa-Rahmat-Ullah" is the most recommended.

15. Performing the pillars in order
This means if one makes Sujud before Ruku' intentionally then the prayer is invalidated. If it is out of forgetfulness, one should return when it is remembered.

Important Note

If one leaves out a pillar, the prayer is not valid.
